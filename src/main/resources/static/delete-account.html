CSI Events app

Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open the CSI Events app and sign in.
  2. Tap the profile icon at the top of your dashboard.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password and confirm.

Can't open the app?

Email harshcodes.sshhr@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my CSI Events account". We will delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email address and password.
  • Your team memberships. Teams you lead are handed to another member, or removed if you were the only member.
  • Any scores you gave as a judge.

Nothing is kept after deletion, except event results that were already published (team names and their final scores, which do not include your personal details).
