Privacy Policy

CSI Events app · Effective 5 October 2026

CSI Events ("the app") helps the CSI student chapter run competitions: students form teams, judges score them and organizers manage events. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash (BCrypt); nobody can read your password.
  • Event activity: the events and teams you take part in, team names you create, and, for judges, the scores you give.
  • Role: whether you are a student, judge or organizer.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising IDs, and it contains no ads or third-party analytics.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To show your teams, team members and events.
  • To calculate and publish event results (team names and scores).

Who can see your data

  • Organizers can see the names and email addresses of registered users so they can assign judges.
  • Members of your team, judges and organizers can see your name as part of your team.
  • After an event's judging is complete, team names and scores are shown to all users.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase and processed by our server hosted on Render. All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account in the app, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deleting your account removes your name, email, password, team memberships and any scores you gave. Teams you lead are handed to another member, or removed if you were the only member.

Children

The app is intended for college students and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
